02 · Biography

A thoughtful, practical engineer.

My engineering identity has been shaped by learning that contribution is not defined by who speaks most in the room. I am naturally reserved, but I have found confidence through careful analysis, persistence, and dependable technical work.

On a fourth-year oceanographic fluorometer project, I developed the data-acquisition system. Seeing that work become a reliable part of the wider system clarified the kind of engineer I want to be: someone who designs practical embedded systems that support a shared goal.

I am continuing to grow in how I communicate ideas, while valuing the focused research and technical execution I bring to a team.

Engineering responsibility

I see technical competence, honesty, integrity, public welfare, safety, and sustainability as inseparable from engineering practice. Reliable systems should be considered alongside their broader consequences.

Collaboration

Specialist contribution.

In team settings, I tend to contribute through independent research and defined technical execution. This task-oriented approach supports targeted, reliable inputs that teammates can analyse and integrate.
